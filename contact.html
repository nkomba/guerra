Ways to share findings

  • Stories & memories — use the story form.
  • Photos & documents — describe them on the photo form, then attach the files to your email.
  • Corrections — the correction form goes straight to the curator.
  • New relatives or branches — the new-relative form.
  • Research leads — the research-lead form.
  • Privacy or takedown requests — see Privacy & Ethics, or email the curator directly.
