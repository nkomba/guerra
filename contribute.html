Share what you know

This whole project depends on relatives like you. A story, a photograph, a correction, a name we're missing, a hint about where to look next — every piece helps. You don't need an account, and nothing you send is published without review.

How submitting works. Because this is a simple, private family site, each form gives you two easy ways to send your contribution to the family curator: open a pre-filled email, or download a neat file you can email or attach photos to. Pick whichever you prefer — both go to the curator.

What evidence helps

You never need "proof" to contribute — but if you have any of these, please mention them:

  • Names, dates, and places (even approximate)
  • A record type and where it's held (parish, civil registry, archive)
  • A photograph, letter, or certificate you can attach
  • Who told you, and roughly when

How review works

  1. You send a contribution.
  2. The curator reads it and may follow up with a question.
  3. It's checked for clarity, privacy, and documentation quality.
  4. It's added with an honest evidence label — or held while we look into it.

Submissions may be lightly edited for clarity, privacy, and documentation quality before publishing.

Please protect living people. Don't submit private details about someone living without their consent. See our Privacy & Ethics policy.
