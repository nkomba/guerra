Welcome

A place for our family to gather what it knows

This site brings together family trees, records, photographs, and remembered stories in one welcoming place. It is built by relatives, for relatives — and for the researchers and descendants who are still finding their way back to us.

Our aim is simple: to trace the Guerra family with care and honesty, to give every ancestor a name and a story where we can, and to be clear about what we know for certain versus what is still a family tradition or an open question.
