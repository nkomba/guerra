Explore & Play

Three simple, friendly ways to get to know where our family comes from. No account, no scorekeeping pressure — just find the village, match the ancestors, and test what you know.

Activity 1

🗺️ Find the Village

Freixo da Serra is a tiny mountain village in central Portugal. Use the buttons to zoom from all of Portugal down to the village itself — then tap the marker to say hello to home.

Map tiles © OpenStreetMap contributors. Location is approximate. Tip: tap the map once to enable scroll-zoom.

Activity 2

🧩 Match the Ancestor

Tap a name on the left, then tap the clue on the right that belongs to them. Green means you got it! (Ancestors come straight from the family tree.)

Activity 3

❓ Family Trivia

Five quick questions about the village, the mountains, and the family's journey. Tap an answer to see if you're right.
